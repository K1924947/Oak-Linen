Our Story

A Boutique Hostel
Built with Intention

The Beginning

Where Craft
Meets Comfort

Oak & Linen was born from a simple frustration: why did budget accommodation so rarely feel beautiful? We believed that warmth, considered design, and genuine hospitality shouldn't be reserved for five-star hotels.

We spent two years curating every element of this space — sourcing oak from sustainable Korean forests, weaving relationships with local artisans, and designing a hostel that feels like a home you wish you had.

Today, Oak & Linen is a quiet landmark in the neighbourhood — known as much for its morning coffee ritual as for its rooms.
